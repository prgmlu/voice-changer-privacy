Voice Changer: Funny Effects: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09

Summary

Your audio stays on your phone. The app has no accounts and no servers of its own, and it never uploads a recording, an imported file or an effect result. The only data that leaves your phone is what Google's advertising SDK collects, described below.

Microphone and audio

  • The app asks for the microphone only when you tap Record or start the live monitor. It never asks at launch.
  • The microphone is used only while the app is on screen and you are recording or monitoring. Nothing records in the background. Recording stops when you leave the app.
  • Effects are processed on your phone. Clips you save are kept in the app's private storage (My clips). Deleting a clip, clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes them.
  • Audio files you import are picked with Android's file picker. The app reads only the file you pick and needs no storage permission.
  • Sharing uses Android's share sheet: a temporary copy goes only to the app you choose. "Save to Music" (Android 10 and newer) copies a clip to the Music/Voice Changer folder on your phone.
  • The app does not clone voices, does not use cloud or AI voice services and does not imitate real people. Please do not use voice effects to deceive or harass anyone.

Settings

Your file format choice (M4A or WAV), the one-time longer-recording reward and the 24-hour ad-free time are kept on your phone.

Permissions

  • RECORD_AUDIO: described above. Needed for recording, not for importing files.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: used only by the ads SDK, to load ads.
  • The ads SDK adds the advertising ID permission, Android's ad-services permissions, and a wake-lock and foreground-service permission for its own background tasks. The app itself never records in the background.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app may collect and use:

  • the Android advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information, how you interact with ads, and crash and diagnostic data.

Google uses this data to show, measure and protect ads, and may share it with its partners. Outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you cannot turn this collection off inside the app. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings. Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Consent

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see Google's consent form on first launch. Where consent is required, ads are requested only as that choice allows. You can change your choice later in the app's settings ("Privacy options").

Children

The app is meant for people aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

We run no server and hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For the data Google holds, use Google's privacy controls at https://myaccount.google.com
